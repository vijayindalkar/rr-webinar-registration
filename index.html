Two Routes You Know. This Webinar Is About the Third.

1

Managing it.

Each option buys time by quieting the pain, but none of them repairs the joint.

2

Replacing it.

A surgeon removes the joint and implants an artificial one. It requires anesthesia and weeks of rehab, and for some people it’s the right choice.

3

Regenerative treatment.

The body repairs everyday wear and tear on its own. With age, fewer repair cells remain, and they have less direction. This treatment adds both back into the joint to help that repair start again.

In your home, by a licensed nurse practitioner, under physician oversight.
